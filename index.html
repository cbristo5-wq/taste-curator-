A deliberate stand against derivative digital artifacts.

This archive documents tangible physical and typographic landmarks that reject standard digital defaults. We explicitly bypass modern "AI slop" UI design tropes—refusing soft rounded corner cards, drop shadows, and low-contrast palettes in favor of strict grid architectures, tactile high-chroma print accents, hairline rules, and structural longevity. Every piece preserved below has been subjected to a rigorous taste filter evaluating physical materiality, structural honesty, and timeless aesthetic clarity.
